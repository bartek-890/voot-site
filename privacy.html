Privacy Policy

Voot has no accounts, no analytics and no servers.

Your progress is stored on your device and, if you are signed in to iCloud, synced through your own private iCloud database so it follows you to a new device. The developer cannot see it, and Voot does not collect, share or sell any data.

Reminders are scheduled locally on your device. Settings → Reset all data removes your progress from this device and from iCloud.

Sharing the daily riddle result uses the system share sheet; nothing leaves your device unless you choose to share it.
